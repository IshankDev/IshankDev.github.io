How I Think
/ 16Philosophy

How I Think

Companies hire architects for their thinking, not their typing. These are the principles I return to when the whiteboard fills up.

01

Simplicity over complexity

The best architecture is the one you can explain in one diagram. Every abstraction earns its keep.

02

Design for scale, ship for today

Model the 10× system, build the 1× that gets there. Avoid speculative generality.

03

Developer experience is a product

If the internal tools are painful, the external product will be too.

04

Automate the second time

Do it once. Script it the second time. Own a platform by the third.

05

AI augments engineers, not replaces them

LLMs are collaborators. Humans still own taste, correctness, and accountability.

06

Architecture is a living thing

Systems evolve. Design for reversible decisions, name your one-way doors.

07

Measure before you optimize

Latency, RPS, error budget. Data beats intuition every time.

08

Secure by design, not by audit

Least privilege. Defense in depth. Zero-trust between services.
